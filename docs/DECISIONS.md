# Decisions

_English | [Português](pt-BR/DECISOES.md)_

> **For whom:** whoever, human or agent, is about to "improve" something that looks arbitrary.
> **What is here:** what is decided and not reopened without the founder, with the date and the reason, and where it is written in the repo.
> **Where to go for the rest:** the design decisions with their rejected alternatives in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#10-design-decisions).

Each line below is already written somewhere in the repo; this page puts them together. Changing
any of them is a new decision with an issue first, not a refactor.
